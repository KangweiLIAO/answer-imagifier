# Privacy Policy for Answer Imagifier - for ChatGPT

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Answer Imagifier - for ChatGPT (the "Extension") creates PNG or JPG images from ChatGPT answers. This policy explains how the Extension handles information.

## Information the Extension accesses

The Extension runs only on `chatgpt.com` and `chat.openai.com`. It accesses the content and layout of the ChatGPT page that is already open in your browser so it can add export controls and render the answer you choose. If you enable the option to include your prompt, it also accesses the prompt immediately preceding that answer.

The Extension may temporarily process text, code, tables, diagrams, formulas, SVG graphics, and readable canvas content that appears in the selected answer. Generated images, citations, embedded pages, audio, and video are excluded from the export.

## How information is used

All image generation happens locally in your browser. The selected content is used only to create the preview and exported image you request. The Extension does not use the content for analytics, advertising, profiling, or model training.

The Extension does not:

- collect or transmit your conversations to the developer;
- operate a remote server or analytics service;
- read cookies, passwords, authentication tokens, or payment information;
- access the ChatGPT or OpenAI API;
- create public sharing links; or
- sell or share personal information.

Page resources needed to reproduce visible content, such as eligible fonts or graphics, may be requested by your browser from their original source while the image is rendered. These resources are not sent to the developer.

## Storage and retention

The Extension does not persist conversation content or user settings. Generated image data remains in browser memory only while the export panel is open. It is released when the panel closes or the page unloads. An image leaves browser memory only when you choose to download it or copy it to the clipboard; the resulting file or clipboard item is then controlled by you and your device.

## Permissions

The Extension does not request optional Chrome API permissions. Its site access is limited by the extension manifest to ChatGPT pages on `chatgpt.com` and `chat.openai.com`.

## Third-party services

Your use of ChatGPT remains subject to OpenAI's own terms and privacy policy. The Extension is an independent, unofficial tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the Extension's data-handling practices change. Material changes will be reflected in this file and identified by a revised effective date.
